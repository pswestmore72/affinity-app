{CHANDLE ASIDE: Crimson italic 10pt · 5% purple wash bg #9B8BB8 · 12pt padding · 16pt margin above/below}
"You're holding three guides. Allow me introductions.

The first — AERALON: A WORLD PRIMER — is for the curious. It tells you about the cosmos: who broke it, what holds it together, and why the gods fight over a world they cannot own. Read this on the road. It will prepare you — or at the very least, worry you appropriately.

The second — the PLAYER'S STARTER GUIDE — is for the brave. It teaches you how to build a character, roll dice, and survive encounters that would prefer you didn't. Your health is actual dice. They break. So might you. This guide helps with the 'might' part.

The third — the GAME MASTER'S STARTER GUIDE — is for the storyteller. The one who builds the world, commands the villains, and decides how much your choices cost. Encounter building, combat flow, skill challenges, and a reference sheet to keep you steady. If you're reading this one, I already like you.

The fourth — THE WINTERMARCH VIGIL — is the story. A two-session adventure set in a frozen forest haunted by the ambitious dead. Five enemy stat blocks, a villain who thinks freezing people counts as saving them, and enough structure to run your first game with confidence.

Together, they are everything you need to begin. A world to explore. Rules to survive it. A story to test both.

I'm Chandle. I sell candles, oddities, and opportunities — and occasionally, I sell the truth. This is the truth: Aeralon is fractured, your dice are your life, and the Balance holds only as long as someone is willing to hold it.

That someone might be you."
{/CHANDLE ASIDE}
