DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

This variant uses a single unified action engine: 2d6 base + all modifiers pooled together, face values add directly, no threshold counting needed.

Core shifts:
• All actions use 2d6 + Prof d6s + Exert d6s (one pool, sum face values)
• Exertion 1s count toward the total BUT burn 1 HD (the price isn't hidden)
• Action types: Auto (no roll) plus four AD tiers — Basic (2), Skilled (7), Expert (13), Legend (25)
• Margin-based effects gate damage, range, and side effects
• Same engine for PCs, NPCs, skills, and initiative
• Exertion/Resolve/Hit Dice remain unchanged

Why this works:
• One mechanic. Everyone uses it. No special rules for NPCs or skills.
• Faster math (sum vs. threshold counting)
• The fracture is visible: you see the 1 that burns you in the same roll that helps you
• Fewer moving parts = more coherent world
• Ability design becomes purely margin-based storytelling

HOW TO ROLL: THE UNIFIED ACTION ENGINE

{H3: Oswald Bold 18pt}
THE CORE LOOP

1. Declare what you're doing (and its type: Auto, Basic, Skilled, Expert, Legend)
2. Roll 2d6 + add all Proficiency d6s + any Exertion d6s (one pool)
3. Sum all face values (1s count!)
4. Compare total to the ability's Action Difficulty
5. Determine Margin = Total - AD
6. Consult ability text for margin breakpoints
7. Resolve any exertion die 1s (each = 1 HD lost, even though it helped)

{H3: Oswald Bold 18pt}
EXAMPLE: QUICK REFERENCE

You attack an Orc with Weapon Strike (Skilled, AD 7).

Roll: 2d6 = 4, 3
Add: 1d6 (Prof 1) = 5
Add: 1d6 (Exert 1) = 1 ← BURNED HD!
Total: 4 + 3 + 5 + 1 = 13

Margin: 13 - 7 = +6

Weapon Strike breakpoints:
• Meet AD (7+): Light 1 HD, Heavy 2 HD ✓
• Margin +1 (8+): Damage +1 ✓
• Margin +3 (10+): Damage + property ✓
• Margin +6+ (13+): Damage + property + knockback ✓

You hit at +6 margin: full escalation. But that exert 1 cost you 1 HD.

The choice was real. The price was visible.

{H3: Oswald Bold 18pt}
ACTION DIFFICULTY TIERS (Level 1 Baseline)

AUTO (No Roll Required):
Purely narrative, or margined effects gate capability. "You automatically catch your balance." But if you want to do it WELL, roll to hit a breakpoint.

BASIC (AD 2):
Routine actions, known targets, favorable conditions. Succeed on almost anything, but margin unlocks bonus effects.

SKILLED (AD 7):
Trained action, uncertain conditions, moderate opposition. Likely to succeed with decent rolls.

EXPERT (AD 13):
Mastery-level, dangerous, skilled opposition. Requires good rolls or positioning bonuses.

LEGEND (AD 25):
Near-impossible, mastery and desperation. Requires near-perfect rolls + exertion.

GM adjusts by ±1-2 for circumstance.

{H3: Oswald Bold 18pt}
EXERTION (THE VISIBLE COST)

• Add extra d6s to your roll (up to Proficiency per action)
• At Level 1: 1 exertion die per action max
• If ANY die in the pool shows 1: You burn 1 HD per die that rolled 1
• Critical: The 1s still count toward your total. You don't lose the benefit.
• Cost is always taken, whether you hit AD or not
• CRITICAL: If you are at 0 Body HD and exert, you automatically FAIL that round's Resolve check. Exertion at critical health breaks you.

Example (continued from above):
Exert 1 rolled 1. You burned 1 HD. But that 1 added +1 to your roll. The fracture is the point.

{H3: Oswald Bold 18pt}
AUTO-SUCCESS ABILITIES (The Fracture Within)

Some abilities don't require rolls — they just happen.

Rally (Auto, Margin-Gated):
• BASE: You rally an ally within 30 yards. They gain +1 to next action.
• To get bonus effects, ROLL to hit a Skilled check (AD 7):
  - Margin +1: They gain +2 to next two actions.
  - Margin +3: They gain +3 to all actions this round.

This teaches a critical lesson: You can always act. But EXCELLENCE requires a roll.

DESIGNING ABILITIES WITH MARGIN BREAKS

{H3: Oswald Bold 18pt}
TEMPLATE

[ABILITY NAME] (Type, AD X)

BASE EFFECT (Meet AD or Auto):
• [What happens when you equal or exceed AD / or happens automatically]

MARGIN +1 (At AD+1 or higher):
• [First enhancement or secondary benefit]

MARGIN +3 (At AD+3 or higher):
• [Significant escalation: damage boost, secondary target, special effect]

MARGIN +6+ (At AD+6 or higher):
• [Cascading: full combo, devastating bonus, or narrative flourish]

{H3: Oswald Bold 18pt}
MARGIN PHILOSOPHY

• **Base effect always matters**: Meeting AD should feel earned
• **+1 to +3 margin is "good"**: Incremental bonuses unlock as rolls improve
• **+4 to +6 margin is "excellent"**: Secondary mechanics fully unlock
• **+7+ is "transcendent"**: Rare, high-skill, perfect execution

Some abilities only have 2 breakpoints (Base + one escalation). Others have 4. Design what the ability needs.

COMBAT BASICS

{H3: Oswald Bold 18pt}
Initiative (Skill Check)

Combat starts with Initiative. It's a Skilled check (AD 7).

Roll: 2d6 + Prof (if Proficiency applies) + any positioning bonus d6s
Margin: Total - 7

Order is determined by margin (highest first). Ties act simultaneously.

Initiative Victor (Highest Margin):
At the start of the first round (before any actions), the winner of initiative can activate ONE class-specific or Path-specific feature that would normally require an action or setup. This is your moment to seize advantage: a Combatant might Rally an ally as a free gift, a Ranger might Mark Prey instantly without rolling, or a Weaver might cast a prepared spell at no cost. GM and player discuss which feature applies (must be an ability or Path bonus that makes sense narratively). This feature still obeys all other rules (damage still needs defense rolls, Resolve checks still happen, etc.), but its activation cost is waived.

Example:
• Fighter rolls: 2d6 = 5, 4 → 9 total. Margin: +2
• Ranger rolls: 2d6 = 3, 5 → 8 total. Margin: +1
• Fighter wins. At combat start, Fighter can (say) activate War-Trained's adrenaline surge from a previous kill to restore 1 HD immediately, or use Rally as a free action to grant an ally +1 to their first attack. No action cost.

Advantage/Disadvantage in Initiative:
• Ambush: +2d6 to Initiative roll
• Surprised: -2d6 from Initiative roll (or auto-lose initiative)

{H3: Oswald Bold 18pt}
Your Turn in Combat

Movement: 10 yards (free, doesn't cost an action)

Actions: 2 per turn
• Attack, use ability, defend, help ally, etc.
• Most are Basic or Skilled actions at Level 1

Free Actions: Drop item, shout briefly, look around

{H3: Oswald Bold 18pt}
ATTACKING

Roll: 2d6 + Prof d6s + Exert d6s (sum all faces)
Compare to ability's AD
Calculate margin
Apply margin breakpoints

At Level 1: Roll 2d6 + 1d6 (Prof), or 2d6 + 1d6 + 1d6 (if exerting).

Example:
Weapon Strike (Skilled, AD 7) with heavy weapon, no exert
Roll: 2d6 = 4, 5 → 9, Prof d6 = 3 → 12 total
Margin: 12 - 7 = +5

Weapon Strike base: 2 HD damage
Margin +1: 3 HD damage
Margin +3: 3 HD damage + property triggers
Margin +6+: 3 HD damage + property + knockback

You're at +5: 3 HD damage + property triggers.

{H3: Oswald Bold 18pt}
DEFENDING

When you take damage:

1. Attacker declares damage amount
2. You roll defense dice (armor first, then body)
3. Roll: 1 defense die per Defense Capacity (L1 = 1)
4. 5+ = survive, cancel 1 damage
5. 1-4 = destroyed, remaining damage goes to next die

Auto-failures: If you run out of rolls, remaining damage destroys dice outright.

Example:
Take 3 HD damage, Defense Capacity 1, Start: 2d8s armor
Roll 1d8 (armor) → 6 = success, cancel 1 damage, armor still 2d8s
2 HD remaining, no rolls left → auto-fail each:
- 1st HD: lose 1d8 armor (2d8 → 1d8)
- 2nd HD: lose 1d8 armor (1d8 → 0, armor gone)
If more damage: starts destroying body HD

SKILL CHECKS AND MARGIN EFFECTS

Skills outside combat also use the unified engine. Many have margin breakpoints.

{H3: Oswald Bold 18pt}
EXAMPLE: PERCEPTION (SKILLED, AD 7)

AUTO: You notice obvious things (a guard at the door, fire burning, someone shouting)

ROLL to get better effect:

BASE (Meet 7): You notice important details (guard's weapon, fire's direction, emotion in the shout)

MARGIN +1 (8+): You notice subtle details (guard's armor type, fire's accelerant, liar's tell)

MARGIN +3 (10+): You notice hidden details (guard's tattoo, fire's age, guard's fear level)

This teaches: Automatic perception exists. Rolling makes you BETTER at perceiving. Margin determines depth.

{H3: Oswald Bold 18pt}
GROUP SKILL CHECKS

When multiple characters attempt a task:

Each rolls 2d6 + Prof (or situational d6s) against the threshold (AD).
Count successes (how many beat AD).

At Level 1, Skilled check (AD 7):
• 3 PCs each roll 2d6 + 1d6
• Results: 9 (margin +2), 5 (margin -2), 11 (margin +4)
• Success count: 2 successes (two beat AD 7)

GM determines: Do you need 1 success? All successes? Or do margins matter?

Usually: 1+ success = basic pass. Margins determine quality.

NPC MECHANICS (No Exertion)

NPCs roll the unified engine but without exertion.

NPC Stat Block (Example — Orc Raider):

Proficiency: 1 (rolls 2d6 + 1d6 for actions)
Body: 5d8s · Armor: 2d6s
Defense Capacity: 1
No exertion.

Weapon Strike (Skilled, AD 7):
Roll 2d6 + Prof d6 (3 dice total). Average ~10-11 — a solid hit against AD 7.
Damage and margins work exactly as they do for PCs.

A tougher NPC simply has more Proficiency or more HD. An Orc Warlord at Proficiency 2 rolls 2d6 + 2d6 (average ~14) and might field 7d8s body.

NPCs NEVER exert. They don't burn HD. They roll the same dice and read the same margins as everyone else — they're defined by their stats, not by special exemptions.

{H3: Oswald Bold 18pt}
NPC ABILITIES WITH MARGIN EFFECTS

Some NPC abilities are gate-kept by margin, just like PC abilities.

Orc Rage (Auto ability):
BASE: Orc gains +1d6 to next attack roll (no margin needed)

Orc War Cry (Skilled, AD 7):
Roll: 2d6 + Prof
BASE (Meet 7): Enemies in 30 yards must pass Fortitude check or be frightened
MARGIN +3 (10+): Frightened enemies take -1d6 to attacks
MARGIN +6+ (13+): Frightened enemies frozen this round (can't act)

This teaches: NPCs aren't simpler — they're just as margin-driven. The table is unified.

Roll: 2d6 = 5, 6 → 11, +Prof d6 = 3, +Exert d6 = 4 → 18. Margin +11.
No 1 on the exert die — no HD burned. Full escalation.

Orc: 1d8 → 0 Body HD. The raider drops.

Kael stands at 0 Body HD, prone, bleeding — but alive. The Orc is down.

Two exertions told the story. The first (Round 1) burned a Hit Die on a rolled 1 — yet that same 1 pushed Kael to full escalation. The last (Round 3) paid nothing and ended the fight. Same gamble, two outcomes, both visible on the table. That is the fracture.

THE PHILOSOPHY OF AUTO-SUCCESS

Some actions don't need rolls. You CAN rally an ally. You CAN perceive basic details. You CAN mark prey.

The roll determines EXCELLENCE, not CAPABILITY.

This teaches: The world doesn't care if you fail the roll. The question is whether you succeed WELL.

Margins are the difference between competence and mastery.

{CHANDLE ASIDE: Crimson italic 10pt · 5% purple wash bg #9B8BB8 · 12pt padding · 16pt margin above/below}
CHANDLE'S ASIDE: ON THE UNIFIED ENGINE

"I've seen a lot of action resolution systems. Most have special cases — 'NPCs do this different', 'skills work like that', 'initiative is separate'. This one doesn't.

Everyone rolls 2d6 + bonuses. Everyone sums face values. Everyone calculates margins. The 1s that burn your Hit Dice are the SAME 1s that help you hit a breakpoint. That's not a bug — that's the design.

It teaches players what Affinity is: A world where power has a cost that's visible in real time. Where there's no hidden math. Where you're making choices at a table with everyone seeing the exact same roll.

And the margin gates? Brilliant. 'Do you PASS or do you pass WELL?' That's the whole game right there."

— Chandle
