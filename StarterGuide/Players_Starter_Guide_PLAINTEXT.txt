WHAT IS AFFINITY?

The gods broke the world. The dead remained. The forests grew teeth.

You are mortal. Your health is a handful of dice — actual, physical dice that shatter when you take damage. When they're gone, so are you. Every swing of a sword, every spell cast in desperation, every choice to push harder than you should — all of it costs something real.

Affinity is a tabletop roleplaying game set in Aeralon, a world held together by tension, not peace. You play adventurers who carry the same fractures the cosmos does — competing loyalties, impossible choices, and the stubborn refusal to break.

This is YOUR story. The dice are your life. Guard them well.

THE FUNDAMENTAL TRUTH

{ILLUSTRATION: Dice depletion visual — full pool > damaged > empty · THIS IS THE MOST IMPORTANT ILLUSTRATION IN THE BUNDLE · teaches core mechanic visually · 30-60mm · ink line art}

There are NO hit points.

You have Hit Dice — actual individual dice that exist or get destroyed.

Example:
• You have 5d10s body dice = five separate d10 dice
• Take 1 damage, fail defense = lose 1 die — now you have 4d10s
• You don't subtract points. You lose DICE.

When your dice run out, you face death. This is the core of Affinity.

HOW TO ROLL

There are two types of rolls: Action Rolls and Defense Rolls.

You'll need: Multiple d6s (two colors recommended) + polyhedral dice (d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20).

{H3: Oswald Bold 18pt}
ACTION ROLLS (d6 Dice Pools)

When you attack, cast spells, or use abilities, roll d6s and count successes.

The threshold depends on the action's tier:

BASIC (2+) — 2, 3, 4, 5 = 1 success each. 6 = 2 successes.
EXPERT (4+) — 4, 5 = 1 success each. 6 = 2 successes.
LEGENDARY (6 only) — 6 = 2 successes. Nothing else counts.

At Level 1, most actions are Basic (2+ threshold).

A roll of 6 ALWAYS counts as 2 successes, regardless of tier.

Example: Basic attack, roll 3d6, get 6, 3, 2 — That's 4 successes.

DEFENSE ROLLS (Armor/Body Dice)

When you defend, roll your actual armor or body dice (d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20).

Defense always uses the same threshold: 5+

• Roll 5+ = Success — die survives, 1 damage cancelled
• Roll 1-4 = Fail — that die is destroyed

DEFENSE DICE SURVIVAL
Roll 5+ to survive. Bigger dice = better odds.

d4   0%  — Absorbs one hit. Always destroyed.
d6  33%  — Light. Fragile protection.
d8  50%  — Medium. Coin flip survival.
d10 60%  — Heavy. Built to last.
d12 67%  — Shields and magic. Hard to break.

Your armor is your lifeline. Invest wisely.

Example: You have 2d8s armor. Take 2 damage, roll both — 7, 3. One survives (7 is 5+), one destroyed (3 is under 5). You now have 1d8 armor.

ACTION RESOLUTION (The Core Loop)

Every action in Affinity follows this pattern:

1. Declare what you're doing (and its tier — usually Basic at L1)
2. Roll your d6s (Proficiency dice + any exertion)
3. Count successes (based on the action's threshold)
4. Check requirements (did you meet the required successes?)
5. Apply effect

{H3: Oswald Bold 18pt}
AT LEVEL 1

{BODY: Crimson Text 10.5pt}
• Proficiency = 1 (you roll 1d6 for most actions)
• Most actions are Basic: 2+ threshold, need 1 success
• That's it. Roll 1d6, get 2+ = you succeed.


{DIVIDER: 0.5pt line #5D5878}

EXERTION (Risk for Reward)

Want more dice? Exert. But there's risk.

• Add extra d6s to your roll (up to Proficiency per action)
• If you roll a 1 on an exertion die: Lose 1 HD from your pool

At Level 1 (Proficiency 1): You can exert 1 extra die per action.

Example:
• Base roll: 1d6 (Proficiency)
• Add exertion: +1d6
• Roll 2d6, get 6, 1 — 2 successes, but lose 1 HD!

Exert. Survive. Fracture.

CHANDLE'S ASIDE: WHY EXERTION BURNS

"You can always play it safe — roll your Proficiency die alone and hope for the best. But safe is boring, and boring gets people killed when the moment demands everything. Every 1 on an exertion die destroys a Hit Die. Intentional. Power has a price. The adventurer who exerts on every attack will hit harder — and die faster. The one who never exerts will be safe — and useless when it matters. The sweet spot is in between. That's where Affinity lives."

COMBAT BASICS

{H3: Oswald Bold 18pt}
Your Turn in Combat

Movement: 10 yards (doesn't cost an action)

Actions: You get 2 actions per turn
• Attack, use ability, defend, help an ally, etc.
• Most things at Level 1 are Basic actions (easy to do)

Free Actions: Drop something, say something brief


{DIVIDER: 0.5pt line #5D5878}
{H3: Oswald Bold 18pt}
ATTACKING d6s (+ any exertion)
2. Need: 1 success at 2+ threshold (for Basic attacks)
3. Hit = weapon damage. 3+ successes trigger weapon properties (see Equipment).

At Level 1: Roll 1-2d6. Any die showing 2+ counts.

Example:
• Attacking with a sword (1 HD damage, Basic tier = 2+)
• Roll 2d6 (Prof 1 + Exert 1), get 5, 3
• Both are 2+ — 2 successes — hit for 1 HD damage
• Extra success could trigger a weapon property if the weapon has one

DEFENDING

{BODY: Crimson Text 10.5pt}
When you take damage:

1. Attacker declares damage (e.g., "3 HD damage")
2. You roll defense dice from outermost layer (armor first, then body)
3. 5+ = that die survives, damage cancelled
4. 4 or under = that die is destroyed, move to next die

AT LEVEL 1

• Defense Capacity = 1 (you get 1 defense roll per attack)
• Roll 1 defense die. If 5+, cancel 1 damage. If not, lose that die.
• Any remaining damage = auto-lose dice from your pool.

Example:
• Take 3 HD damage, Defense Capacity 1, Start: 2d8s armor
• Roll 1d8 (armor) — 6 = success, cancel 1 damage, armor still 2d8s
• 2 HD remaining, no rolls left — auto-fail each:
  - 1st HD: lose 1 armor die (2d8s to 1d8)
  - 2nd HD: lose 1 armor die (1d8 to 0, armor gone)
• If more damage remains, start losing body HD

HIT DICE TRACKING

Your character sheet shows dice pools:

Body HD:  5d10s body dice
Armor:    2d8s armor dice

When you lose dice, cross them off. When they're gone, they're gone.

At 0 Body HD: You're in trouble (see Resolve).

MOVEMENT AND POSITIONING

{BODY: Crimson Text 10.5pt}
Movement: 10 yards per turn (free, split however you like)

ENGAGEMENT: If you're in melee range (~5 yards) of an enemy and move away, that enemy makes a free strike against you — one Basic attack from their stat block. Costs them nothing. Happens every time.

To leave safely:
• Withdraw (costs 1 action): Move your speed, no free strikes
• Or just take the hit and run

POSITIONING BONUSES:
• Advantage (+1d6 to attacks): Favorable position — flanking, high ground, ambush. GM calls it.
• Cover (+1d6 / +2d6 defense): Physical barrier. Light = +1d6. Hard (walls, boulders) = +2d6.
• Concealment (+1d6 defense): Visual obscurity — fog, shadows, dense foliage. Distinct from cover; they stack.
• Brace (give up all movement): +1d6 to your next action

MOVEMENT ACTIONS:
• Charge (1 action + movement): Move 5+ yards straight, attack at +1d6
• Sprint (1 action): Double movement, can't attack
• Withdraw (1 action): Move safely out of engagement

Position matters. Advantage hits harder. Cover keeps you alive. Concealment lets Rangers deal bonus damage AND stay protected. Think about where you stand — the battlefield rewards it.

RESOLVE (Mental Fortitude)

Everyone starts at Resolve Threshold 2 (you need 2+ on 1d6 to hold steady). It rarely escalates at Level 1.

When you make a Resolve check:
• The first time each round that damage destroys a Body HD
• At 0 HD: automatic check each round (no damage needed)
• Certain enemy effects (fear, psychic attacks)

Resolve Check: Roll 1d6 against your current Resolve Threshold.
• Roll at or above RT = You hold (no change)
• Roll below RT = RT increases by 1 (you fall one stage)

Resolve Stages: RT 2-4 (Functional) — RT 5+ (Cracking: no Expert+ actions) — Collapse

Don't worry about Resolve yet. Your GM will tell you when to check.

At 0 Body HD: You're still standing but limited to 1 action per turn. Resolve checks each round.
At 0 HD AND Collapse (fail at RT 6) = death. Until then, you can be saved.

{CHANDLE ASIDE: Crimson italic 10pt · 5% purple wash bg #9B8BB8 · 12pt padding · 16pt margin above/below}
CHANDLE'S ASIDE: WHY RESOLVE ESCALATES

"Resolve starts easy — 2+ on 1d6 to hold. Each failed check makes the next one harder: 3+, then 4+, then 5+, then 6. No tracking. No penalty tables. The spiral tightens itself. Early hits barely register. Late hits are devastating. Characters don't slowly weaken — they hold steady until they can't, then they fracture. That sudden shift from 'I'm fine' to 'I'm in real trouble' is the heart of Affinity combat."
{/CHANDLE ASIDE}

RESTING

Rest (30 min - 8 hours, threatened):
• Regain Proficiency HD (1 at Level 1)
• Lower Resolve Threshold by 1 (minimum 2)
• Armor is NOT restored (no time, no safety)
• Max 2 rests per day

Recover (6+ hours, safe place):
• Regain ALL HD
• Reset Resolve Threshold to 2
• Armor fully restored (time to mend, refit, repair)


{DIVIDER: 0.5pt line #5D5878}
{H3: Oswald Bold 18pt}
ARMOR REPAIR (Field Conditions)

Lost armor dice mid-dungeon with no safe place to Recover? You can attempt a field repair.

Craft Check (Expert, 4+): Need 2 successes
• Requires tools (smith's kit, leather repair kit, or equivalent)
• Takes 10 minutes of uninterrupted work
• Success: Restore your full armor layer
• Failure: Armor stays broken — try again after your next Rest

No tools? No repair. Armor doesn't fix itself. Guard it well.

EQUIPMENT

WEAPONS

All weapon properties trigger on 3+ successes. At Level 1, this usually requires exertion or a lucky 6.

Light (1 HD) — Swift: Make a second attack as a free action (once per turn).
Heavy (2 HD) — Crushing: +1 HD damage.

Ranged (1-2 HD) — Piercing: 1 HD bypasses armor and hits body directly.

Affinity is built on tension. Once you understand why the mechanics feel the way they do, every rule clicks into place.

COMBAT EXAMPLE: THE FULL FLOW

The trail ends at a collapsed bridge. An orc raider stands in the rubble, axe already raised. Neither speaks. Both know.

Kael (Combatant, Level 1) faces an Orc Raider.

Kael: 5d10s body, 2d8s armor, 1d12 shield
Orc:  4d8s body, 2d6s armor, Prof 2, RT 5

ROUND 1 — KAEL ATTACKS

Kael uses Weapon Strike (Basic, 2+) with a longsword (2 HD damage). He exerts for +1d6.

Roll: 2d6 (1 Prof + 1 Exert) — Results: 5, 3
Both are 2+ — 2 successes. Hit!

The exertion die (3) isn't a 1, so no HD burned. Good.

Damage: 2 HD to the Orc.
Orc defends: Rolls 2d8 (Prof 2) — Results: 7, 2
  7 = 5+, success — 1 damage cancelled
  2 = fail — die destroyed from armor layer
Remaining: 1 HD damage. Orc loses 1 armor die (2d6s to 1d6).

Orc is now: 4d8s body, 1d6 armor.

ROUND 1 — ORC STRIKES BACK

Orc swings its Greataxe (Basic, 2+, 3d6, 2 HD damage).

Roll: 3d6 — Results: 6, 4, 1
  6 = 2 successes, 4 = 1 success, 1 = no success
Total: 3 successes. Hit!

(NPCs don't burn HD on 1s — just ignore it and move on.)

Damage: 2 HD to Kael.
Kael defends: Rolls 1d8 (Prof 1, armor layer first) — Result: 3
  3 = fail — armor die destroyed, 1 damage cancelled
1 HD remaining damage — second armor die destroyed.
Kael's armor: 2d8s to 0. Armor gone.

But no Body HD lost — no Resolve check. Armor did its job.

ROUND 2 — KAEL USES OATH-DRIVEN STRIKE

Kael swings hard. Oath-Driven Strike (Basic, 2+, weapon damage + Proficiency HD = 2 + 1 = 3 HD damage on 2+ successes). He exerts.

Roll: 2d6 — Results: 6, 1
  6 = 2 successes, 1 = no success
Total: 2 successes. Hit!

But the exertion die rolled 1 — Kael loses 1 Body HD.
Kael: 5d10s to 4d10s.

Body HD was destroyed — Resolve check!
Kael's RT is 2. Roll 1d6: gets 4. 4 is 2+ = holds. No change.

Damage: 3 HD to the Orc.
Orc defends: Rolls 2d8 — Results: 5, 8
  Both 5+ — 2 damage cancelled
1 HD remaining — destroys Orc's last armor die (1d6 to 0).

Orc is now: 4d8s body, no armor.

ROUND 2 — ORC RETALIATES

Orc is below half HD? No, still at 4d8s. Swings Greataxe again.

Roll: 3d6 — Results: 2, 5, 4
Total: 3 successes. Hit for 2 HD.

Kael has no armor. Defends with body dice.
Kael defends: Rolls 1d10 (Prof 1, body die) — Result: 7
  7 = 5+, success — 1 damage cancelled, die survives
1 HD remaining — Kael loses 1 Body HD. Now 3d10s.

Body HD destroyed — Resolve check!
Kael's RT still 2. Roll 1d6: gets 2. 2 is 2+ = holds. Close.

ROUND 3 — KAEL FINISHES IT

Kael attacks with Weapon Strike. No exertion this time — he's bleeding and can't afford to lose more HD.

Roll: 1d6 — Result: 4
  4 = 1 success. Just enough. Hit for 2 HD.

Orc defends: Rolls 2d8 (body dice now) — Results: 3, 4
  Both fail — 0 damage cancelled
Full 2 HD hits body. Orc: 4d8s to 2d8s.

Orc is below half HD. Rage triggers: +1d6 to attacks.

Kael uses his second action: Weapon Strike again.

Roll: 1d6 — Result: 6
  6 = 2 successes. Solid hit for 2 HD.

Orc defends: Rolls 2d8 — Results: 1, 3
  Both fail. 2 HD destroyed.
Orc: 2d8s to 0 HD. The Orc drops.

AFTERMATH:
Kael is standing at 3d10s body, no armor, RT 2. Bruised but alive. His armor is gone until he can Recover or field-repair it. The exertion gamble cost him a Hit Die — but it also ended the fight a round sooner.

{ITALIC}That's the trade. Every fight in Affinity asks the same question: how much of yourself are you willing to spend to survive?{/ITALIC}

GLOSSARY

{BODY: Crimson Text 10.5pt — stacked list format, bold terms}
Action Roll: Roll d6s and count successes. Used for attacks, abilities, skill checks.
Armor Dice: Polyhedral dice (d6, d8, d10, d12) representing your equipped armor. Destroyed before Body HD.
Basic (2+): The easiest action tier. Roll 2+ on a d6 for 1 success. Most Level 1 actions are Basic.
Body HD (Hit Dice): Your physical health — actual dice that get destroyed when you take damage. At 0 = near death.
Charge: Spend 1 action + movement to move 5+ yards straight and attack at +1d6.
Collapse: Failing a Resolve check at RT 6. You're dead.
Cosmic Focus: The alignment of custodial principles your character follows.
Custodial Power: One of three cosmic paths — The Concordant Halls, The Living Accord, The Curator Arcanum.
Defense Capacity: How many defense rolls you make per incoming attack. (1 at Level 1.)
Defense Roll: Roll a polyhedral die (armor or body). 5+ = die survives and cancels 1 damage.
Domains: The eight areas of expertise — Agility, Awareness, Fortitude, Wilderness, Influence, Lore, Medicine, Craft.
Engagement: Being in melee range (~5 yards) of an enemy. Leaving triggers a free strike.
Exertion: Adding extra d6s to a roll for more power. Rolling 1 on exertion dice destroys 1 HD.
Expert (4+): Mid-tier difficulty. Roll 4+ for 1 success, 6 for 2.
Free Strike: An automatic Basic attack against someone leaving melee engagement.
Group Roll: Everyone rolls and pools successes toward a shared threshold. Default for skill challenges.
HD (Hit Dice): Physical dice representing health. Bigger dice = tougher to kill.
Legendary (6): The hardest tier. Only 6s count, and each 6 = 2 successes.
Milestone: The GM awards level-ups at story milestones. No XP tracking.
Path: A character-defining customization chosen at Level 1, granting passive benefits that scale with Proficiency.
Proficiency: Your base competence. Determines dice rolled and ability uses. (1 at Level 1.)
Recover: 6+ hours in a safe place. Restores all HD, resets RT to 2, restores armor.
Required Successes: How many successes you need for an action to work.
Resolve Threshold (RT): The number you must roll to hold steady (starts at 2). Failed checks increase it.
Rest: 30 min to 8 hours in a threatened area. Regain Proficiency HD, lower RT by 1. Armor NOT restored.
Tier: The difficulty level of an action — Basic (2+), Expert (4+), or Legendary (6).
Way: A class-specific progression path chosen at creation. First node unlocks at Level 2.
Withdraw: Spend 1 action to move out of engagement without triggering a free strike.
