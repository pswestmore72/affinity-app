## Session 1 Mechanical Observations

### What Worked Well
1. **Group Rolls** felt right at 4 players. Nature's Treaty made the Ranger shine as a travel leader.
2. **Encounter 1 (Easy)** served its purpose as a tutorial fight with zero risk.
3. **Encounter 2 (Deadly)** created genuine tension. Armor stripped fast, crystal regen forced strategic priority-setting.
4. **War-Trained kill triggers** provided clutch healing at the worst moments.
5. **Shields (d12)** were the last line of defense and held impressively. The d12's 67% survival rate is noticeable.
6. **Crystal-as-objective** created a split-attention puzzle. Excellent encounter design.

### Issues Found
1. **Lore Check (Expert 4+, need 3, individual):** Mathematically impossible at Level 1 without extraordinary luck. Should be need 2 or made into a Group Roll.
2. **Frost Breath "2d6 cold" damage notation:** Ambiguous. Should say "2 HD cold damage" to match the flat damage model.
3. **Healing Draught wording:** "Restore 1d6 HD each" is ambiguous. Does 1d6 mean "roll to see how many" or "1 die = 1 HD"? Needs clarity.
4. **Watch Bonfire wording:** "+1d6 HD overnight" has the same ambiguity.
5. **Frost Aura vs Cold Resistance:** Completely negated. An encounter mechanic that does nothing might confuse GMs. Consider a note in the adventure.
6. **Crystal HP 15 vs PCs doing 1-2 HD per action:** Takes many rounds. This is probably intentional (forces party to protect the climber), but Sera felt slow.
7. **Armor attrition rate:** Both Combatants lost all armor by Round 3 of Encounter 2. This feels correct — the encounter is SUPPOSED to be a resource drain. But it means Session 2 requires returning to town for Recover.
8. **Hold the Line self-benefit:** Does the caster benefit? Text says "allies" — needs clarification if creator is included or excluded.
