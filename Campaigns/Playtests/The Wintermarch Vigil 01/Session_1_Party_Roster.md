## Pre-Adventure: Magical Armor Ritual

Before departing Coldmarch, Lyris casts **Magical Armor** as a ritual (10 minutes) on herself.
- Grants 2d12s magical armor layer
- This gives Lyris protective dice she otherwise completely lacks

**Lyris adjusted stats:** 4d6s body + 2d12s magical armor
