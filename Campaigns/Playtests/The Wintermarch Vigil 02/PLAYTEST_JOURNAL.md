# Playtest 02 — The Wintermarch Vigil: Journal & Analysis

**Adventure:** The Wintermarch Vigil (GM Starter Guide — post-amendment)  
**Party:** 3 PCs (Combatant, Ranger, Weaver) — minimum recommended  
**Levels:** 1 (Session 1) → 2 (Session 2, milestone)  
**Campaign Duration:** 2 sessions  

---

## Changes Under Test

All amendments from Playtest 01 were active:

1. **Milestone Leveling** — Level 2 awarded between Session 1 and Session 2
2. **DC 2 at Level 2** — Roll 2 defense dice per attack instead of 1
3. **Way Abilities** — Honed Edge, Predator's Patience, Empowered Cantrips
4. **Blessed Arrows (6)** — Force damage, bypass Incorporeal
5. **Healing Draughts** — "restore 1 HD each" (clear wording)
6. **Watch Bonfire** — "+1 HD overnight" (clear wording)
7. **Hold the Line** — "(including yourself)" confirmed
8. **Lore Checks** — Expert 4+, need 2 (was need 3)
9. **Frost Breath** — 2 HD cold (was 2d6)
10. **Thane Regen** — 2 HD/round at Pale Heart (was 1)
11. **Encounter 3 Pacing** — Crystal cracks narrated at 10/5 HP

## Key Findings

### 1. DC 2 Is The Defining Change

The single biggest impact of the Level 2 milestone is rolling 2 defense dice instead of 1:

| Attack | DC 1 (L1) | DC 2 (L2) |
|--------|-----------|-----------|
| 1 HD incoming (d8 defense) | 50% full block | 75% full block |
| 2 HD incoming (d8 defense) | 0% full block (impossible) | 25% full block |
| Armor die survival / attack | ~50% | ~75% |

Armor dice lasted the entire boss fight at DC 2. Brynn ended with full armor. In Playtest 01, both frontliners lost all armor by Round 2. This is the correct feel for "Level 2 adventurers vs the final boss of a starter module."

### 2. Empowered Cantrips Doubles Crystal DPS

Milo's Curator's Bolt went from 1 HD → 2 HD with Empowered Cantrips. Against the crystal (no defense), this meant:
- L1: Milo = 2 HD/round crystal damage
- L2: Milo = **4 HD/round** crystal damage

Combined with Thessa's ~4 HD/round, the crystal took 8 HD/round of focused fire and fell in Round 6 (was Round 8 in Playtest 01). This makes the crystal race feel winnable rather than grueling.

### 3. Honed Edge Creates a Finisher

The Combatant's Honed Edge (+1 HD vs ≤half HP) activates exactly when the boss fight enters its critical phase. Brynn went from dealing 2 HD/hit to 3 HD/hit, and eliminated the Thane in 2 rounds of focused attacks once Honed Edge was active. This gives the Combatant a satisfying power spike at the right dramatic moment.

### 4. Predator's Patience Was Unused

Thessa never attacked from concealment or height advantage during Session 2. The boss arena (frozen lake, open cairn) provides no natural concealment. This Way ability needs encounters with terrain features — forest, ruins, elevation — to function. The Ranger's power spike in Session 2 came from Prof 2 + blessed arrows, not from Predator's Patience.

**Recommendation:** Consider adding a terrain feature to the Pale Heart encounter — fallen pillars, mist cover, or cairn ledges — that enables concealment-based tactics for Rangers.

### 5. Blessed Arrows Solve the Wraith Problem

In Playtest 01, Wraiths were literally unkillable by physical characters (Incorporeal halves physical to 0 at 1 HD). In Playtest 02, Thessa dealt full force damage with blessed arrows. The Wraith went from 6d8 to 3d8 before collapsing. 6 arrows was the correct allocation — 3 used in actual combat, leaving 3 for emergencies.

### 6. 3-Player Minimum Is Viable (With Level 2)

The adventure works at 3 players with the milestone. Session 1 was tight — Brynn solo-tanked and relied on kill triggers for sustain. Session 2 was comfortable. The party composition (tank + ranged DPS + caster) covers all necessary roles. A 3-player party at Level 1 for the boss fight would likely TPK.

### 7. Thane's 2 HD/Round Regen Is Appropriate

The bump from 1 to 2 HD/round regen makes destroying the crystal feel urgent. The Thane fully regenerated his armor each round, forcing the party to solve the crystal puzzle rather than brute-force the boss. Once the crystal fell, the Thane dropped quickly — this is the intended flow.

### 8. Lore Check Fix Validated

At Prof 2, the Expert 4+/need 2 Lore check had a ~36% unassisted success rate (2d6 at 4+, need 2). Milo succeeded on a natural 4/6 roll. Compare to Playtest 01 where Prof 1 made it nearly impossible. The information reward (crystal backlash = 3 HD to Thane) is worth pursuing but not guaranteed.

## Balance Assessment

### Is Level 2 vs Thane Too Easy?

The party took almost no damage and used zero healing in Session 2. Does this mean the encounter is too easy?

**For new players: No.** The boss fight lasted 8 rounds with genuine tactical decisions:
- Break the Frost Wall (2 rounds of resource investment)
- Allocate blessed arrows vs regular
- Choose crystal vs Thane as primary target
- Manage Hold the Line positioning
- React to the Thane's Frost Nova

A new player party will make suboptimal decisions and take more hits. The buffer that Level 2 provides is exactly the margin of error a starter adventure needs.

**For experienced players: Slightly.** A veteran group that immediately targets the crystal and plays optimally will cruise through. The Thane never felt like he could TPK the party. If the adventure is intended for experienced groups, consider:
- Adding a 3rd Wraith (forces more blessed arrow allocation decisions)
- Giving the Thane a phase-2 ability when the crystal breaks (e.g., Frost Nova recharge)
- Increasing crystal HP to 25

### Is Level 1 vs Thane Too Hard?

Based on Playtest 01: yes, for the intended audience. A 4-player Level 1 party barely survived with 1 PC downed and all resources consumed. A 3-player Level 1 party would TPK with high probability. The milestone is necessary.

---

## Recommendations for GM Starter Guide

1. **Keep the Level 2 milestone.** It's the right call. The boss fight is challenging but survivable.
2. **Predator's Patience needs terrain.** Add a note about cairn features (broken pillars, mist) that provide concealment for Ranger players.
3. **Consider Frost Nova recharge.** If playtesting reveals the fight is too easy for experienced groups, giving the Thane a second Frost Nova after crystal destruction would add a final-phase spike.
4. **Blessed arrows count is correct.** 6 covers a 3-4 player party with margin.
5. **Resource pacing is good.** Players enter Session 2 having used 1/3 draughts, which feels right — they've invested some but aren't desperate.

---

## Open Questions for Future Playtests

- **4-player party at Level 2:** Does the 4th player trivialize the encounter? Need to test.
- **Predator's Patience with terrain:** If cairn cover is added, does the Ranger's +2 HD from concealment make the encounter too bursty?
- **Thane's Prof 3 defense vs Level 2 attacks:** The Thane still blocks reliably (3d6 defense). He's tanky enough to survive focused fire for 4+ rounds post-crystal. This feels right.
- **RT escalation:** The party stayed at RT 2 the entire fight. RT never became a factor. For the Starter Guide, this is fine — RT should be a mid-campaign pressure, not a tutorial mechanic. But it means Wraith Resolve checks and Death Wails are non-threatening.
