# The Wintermarch Vigil — Playtest 02 Party Roster

**Playtest ID:** The Wintermarch Vigil 02  
**Version:** Post-amendment (milestone L2, blessed arrows, HtL self-benefit, fixed draughts)  
**Party Size:** 3 (testing minimum recommended size)  
**Starting Level:** 1 (milestone to Level 2 between sessions)  

---

## WHY 3 PLAYERS

Playtest 01 ran 4 PCs. This run tests the minimum (3 players) to validate the adventure still works with a smaller party and to stress-test the tighter action economy.

---

## PARTY ROSTER

### 1. BRYNN — Combatant (Way of the Blade / War-Trained)

**Concept:** Shield-and-sword frontliner, Valkora devotee, ex-Winterwatch soldier  
**Virtue:** Guardian (Concordant Halls)  
**Hit Die:** d10  

**Level 1 Stats:**
| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Body HD | 6d10s (5 base + 1 War-Trained) |
| Armor | 2d8s (medium) |
| Shield | 1d12 |
| Proficiency | 1 |
| RT | 2 |
| DC | 1 |
| Actions | 2 |
| Movement | 10 yards |

**Attack Pool (Level 1):** 1d6 (Prof) + 1d6 (War-Trained, safe) = **2d6** → Heavy weapon = 2 HD  
**Signature Weapon:** Blades (longsword + shield)  

**Abilities:**
| Ability | Type | Details |
|---------|------|---------|
| Weapon Strike | Basic 2+, 1 success | 2 HD, heavy weapon |
| Hold the Line | Basic 2+, 2 successes | 10yd zone, enemies half move, allies (incl self) +1d6 first defense |
| Rally | Basic 2+, 1 success | 30yd, ally +1d6 next action |
| Oath-Driven Strike | Basic 2+, 2 successes | 2 HD + Prof HD, 3+: push/prone |
| Battlefield Awareness | Passive | +1d6 terrain/positioning reads |
| Warrior's Presence | Passive | +1 auto-success intimidation |
| War-Trained | Path | +1d6 safe attacks (blades), +1 body HD, kill trigger +1 HD |

**Level 2 Upgrade (between sessions):**
- Prof 1→2, DC 1→2, Exertion cap 1→2
- Way of the Blade Row 1: **Honed Edge** — Weapon Strike +1 HD vs targets at half HD or less
- Attack pool becomes: 2d6 (Prof) + 1d6 (War-Trained) = **3d6**
- Defense: rolls 2 dice per attack instead of 1

## PARTY SUMMARY (Level 1 → Level 2)

| PC | Class | Body | Armor | Shield | Attack (L1→L2) | Role |
|----|-------|------|-------|--------|-----------------|------|
| Brynn | Combatant | 6d10s | 2d8s | 1d12 | 2d6→3d6, 2 HD | Tank / frontline |
| Thessa | Ranger | 5d8s | 2d8s | — | 1-2d6→2-3d6, 2 HD | Ranged DPS / scout |
| Milo | Weaver | 4d6s | 2d12s (magic) | — | 1d6→2d6, 1→2 HD | Ranged support / force dmg |

**Total Body HD:** 15 (vs Playtest 01's 21 with 4 PCs)  
**Total Armor HD:** 4d8s + 1d12 + 2d12s = 7 dice (vs Playtest 01's ~9)

**Key Difference from Playtest 01:** One fewer PC means:
- 6 actions/round instead of 8
- Less total HP to absorb swarm damage
- No second Combatant to Hold the Line
- Blessed arrows give Thessa force damage option vs Incorporeal

**Pre-Adventure:** Milo casts Magical Armor ritual (7.5 min with Spell-Touched).

**Supplies:**
- 3 healing draughts (restore 1 HD each)
- 6 blessed arrows (force damage, bypass Incorporeal)
- 4 alchemical fire vials
- 1 signal horn
