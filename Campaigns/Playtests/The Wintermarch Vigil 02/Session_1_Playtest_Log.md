### Lore Check (Individual, Lore, Expert 4+, need 2)

Milo attempts: 1d6 → **6** = 2 successes! **SUCCESS!**

The 6 = 2 successes mechanic makes this check achievable (16.7% chance on a single d6). With the fix from need 3 → need 2, this is now passable. Milo learns this is a Frost Focus — destroy the crystal, end the undead.

**OBSERVATION:** At need 2, a single d6 at Expert (4+) has: P(4)=1/6, P(5)=1/6, P(6)=1/6 for success. But need 2 successes from 1d6 means you MUST roll a 6 (only way to get 2 from 1 die). P = 16.7%. Tight but not impossible. With exertion (2d6), P(at least 2 at 4+) = P(both 4+) + P(one is 6) ≈ 25% + 18% = ~36%. Much more reasonable.

## Session 1 Observations

1. **3-player navigation** works fine with Nature's Treaty group bonus (+1d6 to all). Without it, Moderate checks fail ~37% of the time.
2. **Encounter 1 (2 Scouts)** took 3 rounds instead of 2. Appropriate for 3 PCs.
3. **Encounter 2** was the real test. Brynn solo-tanked 8 enemies and survived thanks to War-Trained kill triggers. Without a second frontliner, the swarm pressure concentrated dangerously.
4. **Crystal destruction** took 5 rounds — fast enough that Brynn survived, but only barely.
5. **Lore check (Expert 4+, need 2)** passed on a lucky 6. The fix works — difficult but achievable.
6. **Blessed arrows** not tested in Session 1 (no Incorporeal enemies). Saved for Session 2.
7. **Healing draughts (1 HD each)** — clear and unambiguous. Party chose to save them, which is a risk/reward decision. Good design.
8. **Hold the Line self-benefit** saved Brynn from losing an armor die to Frost Breath. Direct validation of the fix.
