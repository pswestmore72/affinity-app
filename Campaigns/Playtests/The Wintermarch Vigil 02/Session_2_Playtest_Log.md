## Session 2 Observations

### The Level 2 Difference

| Metric | Playtest 01 (L1 vs Boss) | Playtest 02 (L2 vs Boss) |
|--------|--------------------------|--------------------------|
| Encounter duration | 12 rounds | 8 rounds |
| PCs downed | 1 (Kessa to 0 HD) | 0 |
| Healing draughts used | 3/3 | 0/2 |
| Body HD lost (total party) | ~16 HD | ~3 HD |
| Crystal destruction round | Round 8 | Round 6 |
| Thane death round | Round 12 | Round 8 |

**DC 2 is the single biggest change.** Rolling 2 defense dice instead of 1 transforms survivability:
- At DC 1: P(block 1 HD with d8) = 50%. P(block 2 HD) = 0% (only 1 roll).
- At DC 2: P(block 1 HD with 2d8) = 75%. P(block 2 HD with 2d8) = 25%.

Armor dice last dramatically longer. Brynn still had full armor at end of fight.

### Way Abilities in Action

1. **Honed Edge (Combatant):** +1 HD vs ≤half HP turned Brynn into a finisher. He dealt 3 HD per hit to the Thane once it activated. The Thane went from 5d6 to 0 in 2 rounds of focused assault.

2. **Empowered Cantrips (Weaver):** Curator's Bolt going from 1 HD → 2 HD doubled Milo's crystal and combat damage. This was the key to the crystal falling in Round 6 instead of 8+.

3. **Predator's Patience (Ranger):** Not tested — Thessa never attacked from concealment. This Way ability needs encounters with ambush opportunities to shine. In a "charge across open ice" fight, it's unused.

### Blessed Arrows

**VERIFIED FIX:** Thessa dealt damage to the Wraith using blessed arrows (force damage bypasses Incorporeal). 2 HD per hit at full damage vs 1 HD halved to 0 at Level 1. The Wraith went from "literally unkillable" in Playtest 01 to "tough but beatable" in Playtest 02.

6 arrows was the right count. Thessa used 3 (1 Mark + 2 Strikes). A 4-player party might use 4-5. Extra arrows mean the party doesn't feel they need to hoard them.

### 3-Player Viability

The adventure works at 3 players with the Level 2 milestone. Key findings:
- **Session 1 (Level 1):** Tight but survivable. Brynn had to solo-tank and relied on kill triggers for sustain.
- **Session 2 (Level 2):** Comfortable. DC 2 + Way abilities + Prof 2 attack pools made the boss fight challenging but never desperate.
- **No PCs downed.** Compare to Playtest 01's Kessa at 0 HD.

### Resources Remaining

The party finished with 2 unused healing draughts, 3 blessed arrows, 1 alchemical fire, and Overcast. They were never forced to use emergency resources. The Level 2 milestone provides enough power that the boss fight doesn't require full resource expenditure for a competent party.

**IS THIS TOO EASY?** For 3 experienced players: possibly. For 3 new players learning the system: this is the right difficulty. The boss fight still lasted 8 rounds with genuine tactical decisions (wall breaking, crystal targeting, blessed arrow allocation). Nobody died, but the Thane felt threatening.
