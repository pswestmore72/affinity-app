PAGE 1 - WHAT IS AERALON?
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

WELCOME TO AERALON
────────◇────────

This is a world where the gods cannot agree, the dead do not always rest, and the forests think in centuries.

Aeralon is a high-fantasy cosmology built on a single principle:

BALANCE IS NOT PEACE. BALANCE IS TENSION.

The cosmos holds together not because its forces cooperate, but because they push against each other so hard that nothing can move without consequence.

You live in the Mortal Weave — the largest single focal point for all of these forces. Every god reaches toward your world. None may claim it. That contested space is where your stories happen.

────────◇────────
THE SHAPE OF THE COSMOS
────────◇────────

All existence is suspended within the Veil Beyond — a lightless boundary separating reality from unreality. Nothing exists beyond it. Nothing can.

Within the Veil drift the Realms, vast territories shaped by belief, principle, and primordial force.

Between them flows the Ethereal Void — navigable emptiness measured not in distance, but in will and intent.

THE THREE GREAT STRATA

1. THE REFLECTED REALMS — Three mirrored worlds bound to mortal existence
2. THE SOVEREIGN REALMS — Seats of divine authority and principle
3. THE FRACTURED REALMS — Unstable, lawless, or forbidden spaces

PAGE 2 - THE REFLECTED REALMS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

THE REFLECTED REALMS
────────◇────────

Three echoes of one truth — mortality, growth, and ending — bound together in a reflection that cannot be broken without unmaking all three.

Where a mountain rises in the Weave, its shadow falls in the Gloam, and its overgrown twin forest chokes the Verge. What dies in one ripples through the others. What grows without death in one must be paid for elsewhere.

Together they form the Prime Focal Point — the convergence where cosmic law and arcane energy concentrates because belief, death, and growth press against each other without resolution.

· · ─────────────────────── · ·

► THE WEAVE (The Mortal Weave)
Realm of Balance, Memory, and Mortality

This is your world. The core Plane of existence. The only Plane to encompass elements and inhabitants from all other realms. Full of life and death in near equal measure — and this balance makes it unique.

Every god reaches toward the Mortal Weave, yet none may claim it. The Balance exists because they cannot agree.

Crownstags wander ancient forests. River-serpents guard waterways older than kingdoms. Glass-winged hawks hunt at altitudes where the Veil thins. Magic migrates here from every surrounding Plane — and mortals are the only beings trusted to hold the tension by the will of the cosmos.

No being of higher power is allowed entrance into the Mortal Weave. Higher beings routinely bypass these restrictions. But only one deity resides here freely: Aluneth, the great Dragon Turtle of memory and tides, whose waters no one has found twice.

· · ─────────────────────── · ·

► THE GLOAM (The Hollow Marches)
Realm of Death, Endings, and Mercy

A reflection of the Weave shrouded in haze and pale darkness — lit only by a dim ever-present moon and faint distant stars. Where the Weave asks "what will you do?" and the Verge asks "what could you become?", the Gloam asks only:

"What did this all mean?"

This is not a land of the dead. It is the weight of endings, given geography.

The Gloam does not have seasons or governments. It has STATES OF MOURNING that roll across the landscape like weather:

• THE VIGIL — Denial made manifest. Time slows. The recently dead walk beside the living. Wounds that should kill do not. Those caught in deep Vigil may not realize they have died for years.

• THE REMEMBRANCE — Active memory. Grief that honors. The dead appear as they wished to be remembered. Monuments rise from nothing. Names echo from the stones. The Gloam at its most bearable.

• THE RELEASE — Acceptance. The exhale. Mists thin, paths open, and the dead finally pass onward. Melancholy but not painful. The Gloam is most navigable here.

• THE HOLLOW — Grief that has curdled into emptiness. When it dominates, even the moon dims. The dead do not appear — they hunger. Names are forgotten. Purpose dissolves.

Life exists here, though often touched by curse, loss, or loneliness. Vampires, ghosts, revenants — most are not hostile by nature. They are simply unresolved. The truly dangerous are those who have forgotten why they remain.

The Pale Shepherds — psychopomp entities — guide the unmoored dead. They do not judge. They do not comfort. They simply ensure those ready to pass find their way.

· · ─────────────────────── · ·

► THE VERGE (The Tangled Verge)
Realm of Excess Life, Growth, and Wild Magic

The opposite of the Gloam — full of unchecked vitality, biological excess, and generative chaos. Often called the Faerie Plane. Despite its beauty, this is a very dangerous and hostile Plane.

This is not a kingdom. It is an ecosystem that achieved consciousness.

The Verge does not have rulers. It has ECOLOGICAL IMPERATIVES given will — biomes that achieved sentience, pulsing through the realm in century-long waves:

• THE BLOOM — A superorganism of unstoppable growth. When it dominates, forests overtake mountains, flowers choke rivers, and beauty becomes suffocation.

• THE WITHERING — Sentient ripening toward decay. Not winter — the necessary maturation that precedes rest. When it dominates, fruit falls endlessly, leaves blanket the land in gold, and nothing new can grow until the old is composted.

• THE TEMPEST — Unchecked creative violence. Storms that seed forests instantly. Lightning that births new species. When it dominates, the Verge rewrites itself hourly.

• THE BURIED ROOT — Slow-growth consciousness of old forests thinking in centuries. When it dominates, trees become stone-hard, and nothing young can break through the canopy.

Promises here are metabolic bonds. Bargains become symbiosis. The Verge does not negotiate — it incorporates.

PAGE 3 - THE SOVEREIGN REALMS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

THE SOVEREIGN REALMS
────────◇────────

Seats of divine authority where the gods maintain their highest power. Filled with divine magic — similar to arcane magic but more difficult to wield, often requiring aid from stronger beings.

► THE EMPYREAL ACCORD
Realm of Law, Unity, and Sacred Obligation

The largest Outer Plane, home to celestials — unicorns, angels, pegasi, oath-lions, silver serpents. Where the gods of light hold their seats. Nyroth Stonebound. Aubret the Unbent. Beryn the Laughing Dawn. Caerith the Dawnwarden. Korthane the Coinbound.

Travel here is not advised. The creatures and deities hold strict limits on who may enter.

► THE WILD EXPANSE OF THEIWANAR
Realm of Titanic Life and Perfect Nature

A Plane of perfect natural life — vast mountain ranges, wide oceans, frigid tundras, and a rainforest spanning 40% of an entire world's mass. Home to Thymera the Verdant-Mother (Goddess of Life) and Darthun the Coil Below (Lord of Decay). God-apes. Bloom-hydras. Thunder-tortoises.

Open to travel. Extreme caution advised — native creatures are among the strongest ever recorded.

► THE ENGINE OF BAEL-ZAROTH
Realm of Rewritten Obligation and Metaphysical Tyranny

Also known as The Final Obligation, The Depth, or simply The Hells.

This is not a kingdom. It is a malfunctioning metaphysical machine.

Before the Infernal Schism, this realm was a balanced engine of consequence governed by multiple powers. Then Azravael performed the Crown-Breaking — an act that replaced the final line of every contract in existence. All debt, all obligation, all binding now terminates in her.

She did not conquer. She rewrote the fundamental nature of obligation.

The realm exists as a gradient of compression — where metaphysical law progressively overrides physical law:

• SHALLOW DEPTHS — Contracts must still be signed willingly. Negotiation is possible.
• INTERMEDIATE DEPTHS — Intent is interpreted as consent. Ambiguous language auto-completes in her favor.
• EXTREME DEPTHS — Existence itself constitutes agreement. Free will collapses under accumulated obligation.

Entry is forbidden not because the realm is dangerous (though it is), but because entering constitutes acceptance of local jurisdiction. The Engine interprets presence as consent.

Even speaking Azravael's name too often can trigger micro-obligations that scar one's soul.

THE FRACTURED REALMS
────────◇────────

Unstable, forbidden, or primordial spaces — the places "between" all other Planes.

► THE ASHEN WASTE (The Wastelands)
Realm of Pain and Suffering as Truth

A vast unlivable desert ruled by Gephorix the Many-Masked — deity of excess, sensation, and truth revealed through extremity. Most inhabitants live underground. The portal distance to the Mortal Weave is the shortest of any Outer Plane, making it a jumping point for illegal planar travel.

Gephorix does not distinguish between agony and bliss. Both are merely intensity. And intensity reveals what moderation conceals.

Every 38⅔ years, mortals illegally flood here for a massive desert dance — a congregation of millions from across the Planes. Resulted in total guest annihilation only twice throughout history. General survival rate: approximately 43%.

The priestesses of Ilyra the Hearthbound — goddess of home and community, who never manifests — set up hospitality camps in the Ashen Waste during the Dance to tend the wounded, the overwhelmed, and the broken. They are the breath between screams. The pause that makes the next sensation mean something.

Even Gephorix allows it. Because without reprieve, intensity loses its contrast.

► THE ROILING CRUCIBLE
Realm of Primordial Flux and Elemental Becoming

The Plane of Elements — all elements found on any Plane exist here. Central to the Pact of Neutrality, which bars all higher beings from using it as a gateway. Iosyx the Twin-Flame (Chaos, Elements, Change) and Zarun the Unshaped (Freedom, Identity, Becoming) call this home.

Closed to general travel. Visas for scientific exploration are available.

► THE OUTER BLACK (Eternal Dark)
Realm of Absence and Unmaking

Where the Elemental Storm holds all building blocks of the Universe, the Eternal Dark holds nothing — only deep, black space. Almost nothing is known. Creatures brought from here are among the worst terrors released onto any Plane.

Its most powerfull are simply called the Nameless. None have been seen. Only known encounters come from planar meddling of fools.

ILLEGAL TO TRAVEL. Some domains will detain or kill anyone caught traveling to or from this Plane on sight.

THE GODS OF AERALON
────────◇────────

These are not superheroes with portfolios. They are principles that achieved personality. Their conflicts are philosophical, not territorial — and mortals live in the consequences.

GODS OF ORDER & PROTECTION

• NYROTH STONEBOUND — Fighting, friendship, honor. A great Dwarf with a hammer and clasped hands. Will directly act at the beckoning of those who call. Both caring and gruff.

• AUBRET THE UNBENT — Law and righteousness. The only deity-powered being never to have entered the Mortal Weave. A vertical silver line — unbending, uncompromising.

• CAERITH THE DAWNWARDEN — Protection, active mercy, guardianship. She is not a conqueror or judge. She is the one who holds the line so others may flee. Teaches that mercy is not passive — it must sometimes be enforced with a shield.

• KORTHANE THE COINBOUND — Trade, wealth, debt, fair exchange. Unlike Azravael, Korthane's bargains are finite. Debt does not follow the soul. All obligations must eventually end. His temples double as counting houses and neutral ground where even enemies can trade.

GODS OF MEMORY & PASSAGE

• ALUNETH — Memory, tides, continuity. A massive Dragon Turtle (140 feet), the only higher being legally residing in the Mortal Weave. Will offer aid to any creature that asks, usually by answering a question. None have found her twice.

• THALOS OF THE EVEN HAND — Fate, choice, consequence. Does not decide outcomes — ensures outcomes matter. His clergy record decisions, crossroads, and aftermaths.

• ARALYS THE VEILBEARER — Death, final mercy, remembrance. A Moon Elf with dark purple skin and bright white hair. Keeper of light in the darkest places. Chose to wander the Gloam while other gods remain in the heavens.

GODS OF LIFE & CYCLE

• THYMERA VERDANT-MOTHER — Nature, growth, healing, inevitability. Goddess of Life — all life. Appears as a common Elf adorned in wreaths of greenery and flowers.

• DARTHUN THE COIL BELOW — Poison, disease, rot, cycles, necessity. An immense python. Clears death with rot. Seen as evil because worshipers often are — but decay is not malice.

• ILYRA THE HEARTHBOUND — Home, community, survival. Never manifests physically. Presence felt as warmth in shared spaces, as relief after hardship endured together. Worshiped in kitchens, inns, and rebuilt villages. Not in temples.

GODS OF CHANGE & BECOMING

• IOSYX THE TWIN-FLAME — Chaos, elements, dragons. A two-headed dragon of ever changing colors.

• ZARUN THE UNSHAPED — Freedom, identity, transformation. Constantly shifting form. Teaches that identity is not discovered — it is forged repeatedly.

GODS OF TYRANNY & CORRUPTION

• AZRAVAEL — Rewritten obligation, eternal debt, infernal law. She does not sit on a throne — she is the throne, the law, and the signature simultaneously. She does not need you to worship her. She only needs you to owe something to someone.

• GEPHORIX THE MANY-MASKED — Pain, despair, vanity, excess, euphoria, pleasure, sensation. Three heads: the weeping face (suffering), the laughing face (pleasure), the silent face (desire unfulfilled).

• VELKIR THE ASH-TONGUED — Secrets, corruption, whispered power. Offers truths stripped of context, power without perspective. What happens when knowledge loses humility.

THE CUSTODIAL POWERS
────────◇────────

The Weave is not governed by thrones. It is stabilized by paths that mortals choose to walk, generation after generation, until those paths gain weight enough to shape reality.

These paths are known as CUSTODIAL POWERS.

They are not gods. They are agreements made durable through sacrifice.

Each embodies a paired tension that only mortals can hold without breaking the Balance. The other Reflected Realms are overseen by demi-divine entities, but the Weave alone trusts its balance to mortal commitment.

· · ─────────────────────── · ·

► THE CONCORDANT HALLS — Oath & Purpose

An oath alone is hollow. Purpose without oath is chaos. The Concordant Halls exist where those two forces meet.

Who walks the Halls: Paladins whose oaths define their identity. Fighters who carry codes older than the banners they serve. Judges, wardens, shield-captains, and civic guardians.

THE SEVEN CHARGES:
• The Shield — Protection, guardianship, sanctuary
• The Blade — Justice, enforcement, righteous consequence
• The Bond — Loyalty, fellowship, unbreakable trust
• The Crown — Authority, command, burden of leadership
• The Flame — Defiance, renewal, hope against despair
• The Stone — Tradition, foundation, enduring memory
• The Bell — Witness, truth-telling, accountability

Each Charge is vital. None are moral.

A paladin of light and an executioner of darkness can both walk the Halls if their oaths are absolute. The cosmos does not judge the oath — only whether it is kept.

This is why the Halls terrify gods.

· · ─────────────────────── · ·

► THE LIVING ACCORD — Life & Death

The understanding that life without death is stagnation, and death without life is annihilation.

Who bears the Accord: Rangers who understand when protection becomes harm. Druids who prune as often as they nurture. Shamans who speak for land, ancestors, and endings. Death-speakers who carry souls through transition.

THE SEVEN LIVING DOMAINS:
• Breath — Birth, propagation, resilience
• Thorn — Predation, competition, resistance
• Tide — Migration, change, adaptation
• Rot — Decay, recycling, necessary endings
• Hearth — Domestication, settlement, cultivation
• Star — Cosmic influence, seasons, external pressure
• Howl — Catastrophe, extinction, collapse

Each Domain is vital. None are moral.

The Living Accord acts when growth becomes cancerous, when preservation becomes hoarding, or when destruction exceeds renewal.

· · ─────────────────────── · ·

► THE CURATOR ARCANUM — Knowledge & Humility

The Curator Arcanum exist because of a single, brutal truth:

Knowledge without humility always ends the world.

Magic is not forbidden. Magic is tempting. The Curator exist because mortals are curious — and because curiosity survives consequences.

Who walks these Paths: Scholars, weavers, arcanists, seekers. Those willing to learn that the most dangerous spell is the one you cast without understanding why.

THE SIX PATHS OF THE CURATOR:
• The Path of Moments — Time and causality
• The Path of Thresholds — Life, death, and return
• The Path of Unleashed Force — Energy and destruction
• The Path of Mutable Flesh — Form, alchemy, identity
• The Path of Open Doors — Summoning and planar breach
• The Path of Veiled Sight — Divination, secrets, forbidden vision

Each Path represents restraint earned through understanding. None are moral.

PLANAR ARTIFACTS
────────◇────────

Each Realm has an artifact of immense power and terrible cost. These are not loot. They are consequences waiting to happen.

THE TIDEBOUND RELIQUARY (The Mortal Weave)
A living shell the size of a large shield, warm to the touch. When opened near water, answers one question with absolute truth. Cost: A memory of equal emotional weight is erased from the bearer forever. Entire dynasties have collapsed after rulers forgot why they ruled.

"Mortality remembers nothing forever."

THE LANTERN OF LAST MERCY (The Gloam)
A moon-silver lantern that never extinguishes. Reveals the true state of any soul — regret, innocence, or corruption. Cost: That soul is permanently removed from the cycle of life and death. Resurrection becomes impossible.

"Mercy can be final."

THE CROWN OF SEASONS UNENDING (The Verge)
A circlet of living leaves, ice-crystal, and ember-gold. Grants the ability to channel one of the Verdant Excesses across vast lands. Cost: The wearer becomes metabolically bound — Bloom-bearers cannot stop growing, Withering-bearers accelerate decay, Tempest-bearers destabilize reality.

"The Verge does not crown rulers. It incorporates extensions."

THE AEGIS OF SHARED BURDEN (The Empyreal Accord)
A radiant shield etched with names that fade and reappear. Any harm dealt to the bearer is divided equally among all sworn allies. Cost: Those bound cannot refuse the pain once sworn. Entire armies have died willingly rather than allow a single champion to fall alone.

"Righteousness demands sacrifice."

THE LEDGER OF ETERNAL DEBT (The Engine of Bael-Zaroth)
Not a book — a living document that updates itself across all instances. Every soul's contract history is recorded. Attempting to read your own entry causes the text to expand infinitely until the reader realizes they owe far more than they remember agreeing to.

"Obligation outlives death, memory, and even the gods' mercy."

THE MASK OF A THOUSAND AGONIES (The Ashen Waste)
A fractured helm that screams softly when worn. Converts pain — physical or emotional — into immense power. Cost: Each use permanently scars the soul. Every recorded bearer eventually removed the mask. None survived long after.

"Pain teaches what it does not destroy."

THE NULL RELIQUARY (The Outer Black)
An object that cannot be described the same way twice. Anything placed within is erased from existence, memory, and fate. Cost: Even the gods forget what is lost. Historical records show gaps where entire wars should be.

"What is forgotten was never safe."

STORIES FROM AERALON
────────◇────────

These fragments are drawn from the living history of the cosmos. They are not lore dumps. They are the kinds of stories your characters might hear in taverns, read in forbidden libraries, or discover firsthand.

· · ─────────────────────── · ·

THE THIRTY-EIGHTH CONGREGATION
(From the testimony of Chronicler Vedris Halfmoon, Curator Arcanum)

"The thing you need to understand — and I say this knowing full well that understanding will not stop you — is that we knew people were going to die. Not might die. Not could die. Would. Certainly. Inevitably. In quantities that make 'party casualty' feel like a category error. We went anyway."

Every 38⅔ years, the Ashen Waste opens portals across every Plane. Tens of thousands flood in. What follows is a twenty-day rite of intensity that Gephorix the Many-Masked did not merely tolerate — he ordained it as the highest sacrament of his faith.

"A man in mismatched armor stumbled past me, grinning with teeth filed to points. 'First time?' he asked. 'You'll love it. Or you won't survive it. Both feel the same by hour three.'"

Survival rate: approximately 43%.

And yet the priestesses of Ilyra set up camp in the middle of it. Cool water. Actual shade. The simple miracle of someone willing to listen.

"We are the breath between screams," one priestess explained. "The pause that makes the next sensation mean something. Without comfort, intensity loses its contrast. Without hope, despair has no weight."

"Half these people came because they have nothing left to lose. The other half came because they've never lost anything and wanted to know what it felt like. Ilyra doesn't judge any. She serves. She comforts. That's the whole of her teaching — that comfort isn't weakness, that community isn't cowardice, that sometimes the most radical act of faith is not pursuing the limits of transcendence."

Gephorix and Ilyra, sharing the same desert. Intensity and comfort, side by side. Neither able to claim the whole truth.

· · ─────────────────────── · ·

THE CROWN-BREAKING

No one speaks the full name of what Azravael did. It is referred to only as the Crown-Breaking — the moment one will rewrote reality.

Before: The Engine of Bael-Zaroth was a balanced system of infernal function, governed by multiple powers whose authority derived from purpose.

After: All debt, all obligation, all binding in existence terminates in Azravael. She did not destroy the other powers. She amended their foundational code so that every path of obligation eventually routes to her name, whether the original parties know it or not.

The Crown-Breaking sent a shockwave across the cosmos. Beings standing too close to the fracture points — the places where reality thinned as infernal law propagated — were corrupted mid-existence. These became the Schism-Scarred: not a created people, but living proof that one will can rewrite reality without destroying it.

The Mortal Weave, as the only unbiased party, was tasked with creating an elite force — the Interplanar Travel Network — to ensure compliance and prevent another reality-altering event.

She does not need you to worship her. She only needs you to owe something to someone.

· · ─────────────────────── · ·

THE QUESTION OF ALUNETH

Aluneth is a Dragon Turtle roughly 140 feet long. She dwells in the waters of the Mortal Weave — the only higher being legally residing there. Her domains are memory, tides, and continuity.

She will offer aid to any creature that asks, usually by answering a question.

No one has found her twice.

Some have pilgrimaged their entire lives, following tidal patterns and lunar phases to the places where river meets sea. A few succeed. Most do not. Those who find her report one consistent detail: she did not give them the answer they sought. She gave them the answer they needed.

Among her clergy, deliberate historical revision is considered a grave spiritual offense. Under the full moon, names of those lost at sea must be spoken aloud annually — or risk fading from communal memory.

"Mortality remembers nothing forever."

SEEDS FOR YOUR TABLE
────────◇────────

These hooks work in any game system. They are entry points into Aeralon's tensions.

────────◇────────

THE FORGOTTEN DEBT
A merchant in your city has been acting strangely — signing contracts he cannot remember agreeing to, giving away assets to strangers who present paperwork he does not recognize. Investigation reveals the debt originates from an ancestor's signature three generations ago. The Ledger of Eternal Debt has updated. Azravael's agents are coming to collect.

THE MOURNING SHIFT
A frontier village reports that weather has stopped making sense. Frost in summer. Rain that falls upward. Investigation reveals the Gloam's Mourning States are bleeding into the Mortal Weave — someone nearby has died so badly that grief itself has become geography. Finding and resolving the death is the only cure.

THE BLOOM AMBASSADOR
A fey creature arrives in town claiming diplomatic immunity. It smiles too wide, speaks in pollen, and offers gifts that make gardens explode overnight. It says the Bloom is extending an invitation of friendship. The Verge does not have friends. It has symbiotes.

THE SILENT CONGREGATION
A cult has been growing in the underground — worshipers of Gephorix who have discovered that moderate, sustained pain creates a more reliable connection to their god than dramatic acts. They are not violent. They are not breaking laws. But the town is changing. People are seeking them out. The priestesses of Ilyra arrive, quietly set up a soup kitchen next door, and wait.

THE SECOND SCHISM
An arcanist has discovered a fragment of the original infernal law — from before the Crown-Breaking. If restored, it could undo Azravael's amendment. The Curator Arcanum want it destroyed. The Concordant Halls want it studied. The Engine wants it back. And someone has already begun writing a new Crown-Breaking, using the original as a template.

THE LAST TIDE
Coastal communities report that Aluneth has been spotted — for the first time in living memory — swimming in circles around a single island. She does not respond to hails. She does not answer questions. She is guarding something. Or mourning something. Or both.

────────◇────────

[NOTE: These hooks are system-agnostic. If using Affinity: Fracture, Level 1-3 parties can engage with THE MOURNING SHIFT or THE SILENT CONGREGATION immediately. THE FORGOTTEN DEBT and THE BLOOM AMBASSADOR suit Level 2-5. THE SECOND SCHISM and THE LAST TIDE are campaign-scale arcs.]

MAKING AERALON YOURS
────────◇────────

This primer scratches the surface. The full cosmology contains detailed planar guides, expanded deity profiles, the complete Custodial Powers framework, and mythology that stretches back to the moment before the Covenant of Balance.

But you don't need any of that to start.

You need one truth:

BALANCE IS NOT PEACE. BALANCE IS TENSION.

Every story in Aeralon comes from that tension:
• A god of comfort sharing a desert with a god of pain
• A cosmic law rewritten by a single will
• A Dragon Turtle who answers questions no one thought to ask
• Custodial Powers maintained by mortal hands because the gods cannot be trusted
• A world where the dead do not always rest, the forests think, and every contract has fine print

The Mortal Weave is contested ground. Your characters live in the space where all these forces press against each other.

What they do with that pressure is your story.
