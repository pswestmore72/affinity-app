## Critical Errors Fixed

### 1. **Initiative Mechanics (Page 15 - Quick Reference)**
**Error**: Listed initiative as "Roll 3d6, count successes (5+ = 1, 6 = 2)"  
**Correction**: Changed to "Roll 3d6, SUM the total (range 3-18)"  
**Impact**: CRITICAL - Initiative uses summed totals, not success counting

### 2. **Success Threshold Table (Page 15 - Quick Reference)**
**Error**: Generic success table without tier context  
**Correction**: Added complete tier-based success threshold table showing Basic through Ultimate tiers  
**Impact**: HIGH - Prevents confusion about different action tier thresholds

### 3. **NPC Resolve Thresholds (Page 1 & 2)**
**Error**: All enemy tiers listed with RT: 2  
**Correction**: Updated to match official tiers:
- Trivial: RT 5
- Basic: RT 5
- Advanced: RT 4
- Expert: RT 3
- Legendary: RT 2

**Impact**: HIGH - Enemies were significantly more resistant to morale/social effects than intended

### 4. **Example Enemy Stat Blocks (Page 2)**
**Error**: Goblin Skulker (Trivial), Orc Raider (Basic), Animated Statue (Basic) all had RT: 2  
**Correction**: Updated all to RT: 5 (appropriate for their tiers)  
**Impact**: MEDIUM - Example enemies now match tier guidelines

### 5. **NPC Template (Page 2)**
**Error**: Template said "RESOLVE THRESHOLD: 2 (starts at 2, escalates on failed checks)"  
**Correction**: Changed to "RESOLVE THRESHOLD: Varies by tier (Trivial/Basic: 5, Advanced: 4, Expert: 3)"  
**Impact**: MEDIUM - GMs creating custom enemies will now use correct RT values

### 6. **Session Checklist Reminder (Page 15)**
**Error**: Said "Roll d6s, 5+ is success, 6 is two"  
**Correction**: Changed to "Roll d6s. Count successes by tier (usually Basic 2+ at Level 1, and 6 = 2 successes always)."  
**Impact**: LOW - Clarifies tier-based system for GM reminders
